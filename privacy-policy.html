Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. What Data We Collect

When you interact with Tensor Academy, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, or messaging handle when you reach out to us.
  • Certificate Verification Data: The Certificate ID you enter on our verification page, used solely to retrieve and display public certificate information.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected automatically for site improvement.

2. Why We Collect This Data

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide student support.
  • To enable certificate verification and display only the relevant public academic record.
  • To maintain and improve the performance and security of our website.

3. How Verification Data Is Displayed

When a Certificate ID is verified, we display only the publicly intended information associated with that certificate: student name, father's name, program, certificate details, and grade. No private contact information, sensitive personal data, or hidden records are ever shown.

4. How Student Privacy Is Protected

We are committed to protecting student privacy. Personal phone numbers, private addresses, and other sensitive details are never stored or published. Student profiles only appear after a valid certificate verification and contain only pre‑approved academic information.

5. How Contact Data Is Used

Any contact information you provide (email, messaging accounts) is used exclusively for direct communication with Tensor Academy. We do not sell, share, or use your contact data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

6. Data Retention

Contact messages are retained only as long as necessary to provide support. Certificate records are retained indefinitely as part of our transparent academic verification system.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at tensor.ai.academy@gmail.com.
