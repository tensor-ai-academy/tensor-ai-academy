Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Tensor Academy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and services, including certificate verification and contact forms.

1. Information We Collect

Certificate Verification Data

When you use our Certificate Verification tool, you provide a Certificate ID. This ID is checked against our public records to confirm authenticity. We do not store, log, or share the Certificate IDs you enter. The verification process is performed entirely in your browser and no data is transmitted to our servers.

Contact Data

If you contact us via email or Telegram, we will receive the information you choose to share (such as your name, email address, or Telegram username). We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and do not share it with third parties.

Local Storage for Preferences

Our website stores two preferences locally in your browser using localStorage:

  • Your preferred language (English or Persian/Dari)
  • Your preferred theme (Light or Dark mode)

This information stays only on your device. We do not have access to it, and it is never sent to any server. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

2. How We Use Information

Any information you voluntarily provide through contact methods is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide support
  • Facilitate course registration and communication
  • Improve our educational services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

3. External Links

Our website contains links to external platforms such as Telegram. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies when you visit them.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we receive. However, no method of electronic communication is 100% secure. We recommend that you avoid sending sensitive personal information through unsecured channels.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
