Website Policy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Tensor Academy website and its certificate verification features.

Last updated: July 2026

Privacy Certificate Verification Data Contact Data Local Storage External Links Updates

Privacy

Tensor Academy respects the privacy of visitors and aims to handle information in a reasonable and transparent manner. This website is primarily an informational and educational platform for the academy and its services.

The website does not intentionally request unnecessary personal information through the public pages. Information provided voluntarily by visitors, such as when contacting the academy, is used for the purpose for which it was provided.

Certificate Verification Data

The certificate verification system allows visitors to enter a Certificate ID and access the corresponding verification page when a matching record is available.

A certificate verification page may display information associated with a certificate record, such as the certificate holder's name, certificate ID, course, level, instructor, duration, dates, score, grade, status, and listed achievements.

Only information intended to be included in the public certificate verification record should be displayed on a verification page. The structure of the system is designed to support unique Certificate IDs and dedicated verification URLs.

Contact Data

If you contact Tensor Academy through the published contact methods, such as email or Telegram, you may voluntarily provide information needed to communicate with you.

Contact information is used for communication related to your inquiry or request. Visitors should avoid sending sensitive information unless it is specifically necessary for the intended communication.

The current public contact information for Tensor Academy includes:

  • Email: tensor.ai.academy@gmail.com
  • Telegram: @Tensor_academy_maneger

Local Storage for Language and Theme

The website may use the browser's local storage feature to remember your preferred language and visual theme.

The language preference is used to remember whether you selected English or Persian / Dari. The theme preference is used to remember whether you selected Light Mode or Dark Mode.

These preferences are stored locally in your browser and are intended to improve your experience when you return to the website. They are not intended to be used as a substitute for server-side account or identity management.

You can clear locally stored website data through your browser settings. Clearing this data may reset your saved language and theme preferences.

External Links

The website may contain links to external services and platforms, including Telegram, Google Meet, Discord, Google Fonts, GitHub Pages, or other third-party resources.

External websites and services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Tensor Academy does not control the privacy policies or practices of third-party websites and services.

Visitors should review the applicable policies of external services before providing personal information or using those services.

Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, certificate verification system, contact methods, or related practices change.

When an update is made, the latest version will be published on this page. The date shown near the beginning of the policy may be updated to indicate when the policy was last revised.

Questions About Privacy

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled in connection with the website, you may contact Tensor Academy using the published contact information.
