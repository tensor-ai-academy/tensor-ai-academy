Founder & Director

Mohammad Omar Omari

Founder & Director

Educational Background

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science — University of Kabul
  • Master’s degree in Computer Science — University of Tehran
  • Faculty: Machine Intelligence and Robotics

Mohammad Omar Omari is the Founder and Director of Tensor Academy, an innovative online learning platform dedicated to delivering high-quality education for everyone.

He graduated with a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Kabul University and is pursuing his Master’s degree at the University of Tehran, Faculty of Machine Intelligence and Robotics.

With expertise in various fields of technology and artificial intelligence, including deep learning, machine learning, prompt engineering, advanced use of chatbots, and working with AI agents, as well as strong skills in Python, C++, Java, JavaScript, and C#, he decided to establish an online academy to share knowledge and practical experience with learners in these areas.

He has worked to make this educational opportunity accessible to students across Afghanistan and beyond, regardless of location or academic background.

He specializes in designing modern educational systems, intelligent platforms, user-centered digital products, and innovative learning experiences.

Beyond technology, Mohammad Omar Omari is committed to education, innovation, digital transformation, personal growth, and helping young people unlock their full potential through modern learning.
