Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Tensor Academy.

1. How can I verify a certificate? +

Visit the Certificate Verification page, enter the Certificate ID printed on your certificate, and click Verify. If the certificate is valid, its details will appear instantly.

2. Are the courses fully online? +

Yes, all Tensor Academy programs are fully online. You can study from anywhere at your own pace within the structured learning path.

3. How do I enroll in a program? +

You can enroll by contacting us through any of the official channels listed on the Contact page. We will guide you through the enrollment process.

4. Do all programs include a certificate? +

Yes, upon successful completion of a program, you receive a verifiable certificate with a unique Certificate ID.

5. How can I contact the academy? +

You can reach us via email at tensor.ai.academy@gmail.com, on X (@Tensor_Academy), Discord (tensor_academy), or LinkedIn. Full details are on the Contact page.
